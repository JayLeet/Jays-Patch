Jay's Patch 1.9.0 Beta 4 includes everything added during the 1.9 beta so far.

## Beta game modes

- **Greedy Whalebuffet** lets every player submit private character preferences at the same time. The Storyteller reviews the final setup before starting the game.
- **Draft Buffet** gives players private turns with 3, then 2, then 1 character choice while the remaining setup adjusts around earlier picks.
- Both modes now have clearer setup warnings, safer Reset Game handling and complete personal Grimoire support.

## Character tools

- **Wraith support** gives the Storyteller Closed, Peek and Eyes Open choices at night. The Wraith now uses the official ability text.
- **Spy and Widow Grimoires** privately show those players the correct character information.
- **Boomdandy Final Three** removes the other chairs, eliminates non-finalists and lets the survivors vote by standing near a player.
- Greedy's 20 adjusted abilities appear in its role display and Grimoire dialog tooltips without changing other game modes.
- The Vizier entrance now uses the King's jingle with each note stepping downward instead of upward.

## Fun features

- **Paint Guns** fire visible snowballs and cover connected full blocks with temporary, non-destructive colour. The normal gun uses your seat colour; the Rainbow Paint Gun uses different bright colours and now has a fully rainbow name.
- Paint follows the surface it hits, copies nearby light and disappears after 20 seconds. Hitting a player creates a larger splash with particles and a custom sound.
- **Hot Potato** now plays one heartbeat per second during its final 10 seconds, rising in pitch until the Imp pops. Starpasses use a flaming pitchfork trail and name both players in chat.

Made for Sybillian's Blood on the Clocktower 1.5.4 on Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.10. Back up your `world` and `config` folders before installing.
