Jay's Patch 1.9.0 makes the complete 1.9 beta line official.

## Two new ways to play

- **Greedy Whalebuffet** lets everyone privately submit their favourite characters at the same time before the Storyteller builds the final game.
- **Draft Buffet** gives each player a private turn to choose from 3, then 2, then 1 character while the game changes around earlier picks.

## New character moments

- **Wraith** players can keep their eyes closed, peek from home or sneak out with the Storyteller at night.
- **Spy and Widow** players can privately see the true Grimoire.
- **Boomdandy Final Three** turns an execution into a last showdown where the survivors vote by standing near a player's seat.
- Greedy's 20 adjusted abilities appear wherever players read those characters during a Greedy game.

## Mess around between games

- **Paint Guns** let you fire colourful paint around the Town Square and splat other players. Use your seat colour or go wild with the Rainbow Paint Gun.
- **Hot Potato** lets you starpass the Imp with flaming pitchfork shots while its heartbeat builds toward the pop.
- The **Vizier** gets a descending version of the King's dramatic entrance jingle.

## Safer public servers

- YAWP protection now covers buckets and the remaining block-changing interactions so ordinary players cannot modify the world.
